Elektrolyseure

Green hydrogen is produced by electrolysis. Alkaline electrolysis (AEL) and proton exchange membrane (PEM) electrolysis are currently the most widely used methods, with AEL efficiency ranging from 60% to 75% and PEM efficiency at 70%. SOEC and AEM electrolysis are currently under development.
