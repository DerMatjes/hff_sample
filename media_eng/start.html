Hydrogen For Future

is our initiative to achieve rapid, global defossilization through the mass production of Green Hydrogen, going beyond purely green electric energy. We concretely demonstrate how this can be achieved. Given that Green Hydrogen is currently produced only on a low-megawatt scale — not on a gigawatt scale — making it expensive and therefore uneconomical, we focus on solving the scaling volume problem. In this way, we can not only halt global warming but also become independent from fossil fuels. Pilot projects have already demonstrated that the production, storage, and transport of hydrogen are technically refined, making the industrial development of a green hydrogen economy feasible.

Campaign

We call on politics and industry to immediately commit all their resources to the greenhouse gas-free green hydrogen economy in order to save the climate.
