Demand for Green Hydrogen

Demand for Green Hydrogen worldwide for...

the Aviation Industry:

Demand of energy worldwide due to Aviation:

\[ 3{,}48 \cdot 10^{11}\,l \cdot 9{,}5\,\frac{kWh}{l} \] \[ = 3{,}306 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh \]

Demand for Green Hydrogen due to the production of Green Kerosene worldwide:

\[ \frac{3{,}306 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh}{0{,}7} \] \[ = 4{,}723 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh \]

\[ \approx 1{,}42 \cdot 10^{11}\,kg = 1{,}42 \cdot 10^{8}\,t \]

This amount corresponds to the production of approximately 1.420 GW in plants or 47 factories with 30 GW capacity.

the Automotive Industry:

Under the assumption that by 2034 50 % of the world's automotive traffic will be electrified, the remaining 50 % would require green gasoline.

  • Number of Passenger Cars Worldwide: \(1{,}312 \cdot 10^9\)
  • Average Driving Distance: 12.000 km/Year
  • Average Fuel Consumption: 7,7 l/100 km
  • Energy in Gasoline: 9,7 kWh/l

Gasoline demand worldwide:

\[ 1{,}312 \cdot 10^9 \cdot 0{,}5 \cdot 12.000 \cdot \frac{7{,}7}{100} \] \[= 606.144.000.000\,l \]

Energy Demand:

\[ 6{,}06144 \cdot 10^{11}\,l \cdot 9{,}7\,\frac{kWh}{l} \] \[= 5{,}88 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh \]

Demand for Green Hydrogen:

\[ \frac{5{,}88 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh}{0{,}7} \] \[= 8{,}399 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh \]

\[ \approx 2{,}52 \cdot 10^{11}\,kg = 2{,}52 \cdot 10^{8}\,t \]

This amount corresponds to the production of approximately 2.520 GW in plants or 84 factories with 30 GW capacity.

the Steel Industry:

Worldwide Steel Production 2023: 1.89 Billion Tons

Energy Demand: 1.900 kWh per ton of Green Steel

Total Energy Demand:

\[ 1.890.000.000 \cdot 1900\,kWh \] \[ = 3{,}591 \cdot 10^{12}\,kWh \]

Energy Content of Green Hydrogen:

\[ 33{,}33\,kWh/kg \]

This results in a demand for Green Hydrogen of:

\[ \approx 1{,}078 \cdot 10^{11}\,kg = 1{,}078 \cdot 10^{8}\,t \]

This amount corresponds to the production of approximately 1.077 GW in plants or 36 factories with 30 GW capacity.

Conclusion:

For these three sectors alone (Aviation, Automotive, Steel) there is a minimum demand for 5.017 GW of Electrolyzers which is about 167 factories with a capacity of 30 GW.
